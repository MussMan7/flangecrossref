Find the breast-pump flange size that actually fits

FlangeCrossRef is a plain reference for breast shields (flanges): how to measure in millimeters, which sizes each brand actually sells — Spectra, Medela, Motif, Lansinoh, Elvie, Willow, Zomee, Philips Avent, and every other pump we can source, when the same printed mm is not interchangeable across brands, and which adapters / replacement parts people shop for when suction fails. No sign-up, no pop-ups.

Before anything elseFlange size is based on nipple diameter in mm, not bra cup size. The 24 mm pair that shipped with most pumps is a default, not a fit guarantee. Brands publish different mm ladders and different “add X mm to your measurement” starting rules — treat every brand’s number as that brand’s tunnel, not a universal standard. How this site works

  • Sizes come from published sources — manufacturer pages, DME size charts, or clearly attributed references — and each table says where its numbers came from.
  • We don’t guess. If we could not confirm a size or compatibility claim, the cell says so.
  • No medical advice. Fit checks are quoted from manufacturers. Persistent pain or supply concerns belong with an IBCLC or clinician — not a webpage.
