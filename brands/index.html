Every pump brand we can source — not just Medela and Spectra

Directory · Updated Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · Not medical advice

ScopeFlangeCrossRef is a cross-reference for flange (breast shield) sizes and replacement parts across pump brands. Medela and Spectra are two of the most-searched names, not the whole catalog. A printed millimeter is that brand’s tunnel, not a universal size. Dedicated brand pages ship as we can verify each maker’s own size list.

This page lists brands already documented on manufacturer sizing pages or on Aeroflow Breastpumps’ Flange Size Charts PDF (accessed Nov 5, 2026). We do not copy Aeroflow’s full nipple-diameter recommendation grid. In-box and extra OEM sizes below are attributed to that PDF or to the brand’s own guide. If a brand is missing, it is next in line as soon as we can open an official size list — not because it is out of scope.

Manufacturer size guides we opened directly

BrandWhat the brand’s own page saysSource
Motif Measure nipple diameter in mm (no areola); add 4 mm (their example: 20 mm nipple → 24 mm flange). Measure both sides. Motif: Finding Your Flange Size
Elvie Pump and Elvie Stride (not Elvie Stride 2) Up to 12 mm → 15 mm cushion + 24 mm shield; 13–15 mm → 17 mm cushion; 16–17 mm → 19 mm cushion; 18–19 mm → 21 mm shield; 20–22 mm → 24 mm shield; 23–26 mm → 28 mm shield. 24 mm shields come in the kit. Elvie flange & insert size guide
Willow Flanges in 21, 24, and 27 mm. If you measure equal to or smaller than 21 mm, inserts in 13, 15, 17, 19, and 21 mm snap into 24 mm flanges and containers. Willow sizing hub
Spectra Baby USA Measure before pumping or nursing; add about 3–5 mm. Sizes named on their blog include 20, 24, 28, and 32 mm; kits often include 24 and 28 mm. Spectra: Finding Your Flange Size · S1/S2 parts page
Medela Measure at the base and add about 4 mm, then pick the closest PersonalFit / Flex shield. See the measure guide for the full US size list from Medela’s page. Medela Room to Move guide

Also in scope (pages as soon as we can source them)

These names appear in Aeroflow’s pump shop or as brands Aeroflow says it carries, or they are commonly searched wearable/portable pumps. They belong in this directory. We will not invent a millimeter list for them.

  • Ameda and Evenflo — Aeroflow’s insurance coverage pages say they carry these alongside Motif, Medela, Spectra, Lansinoh, and Freemie.
  • Eufy (S1 Pro, S2, S2 Pro) — listed in Aeroflow’s breast-pump shop.
  • Motif Aura Glow and Motif Roam — listed in Aeroflow’s shop (Aura sizing starts from the Motif Aura row above until we open model-specific OEM pages).
  • Zomee Fit wearable and Zomee Mother’s Nature H1 — listed in Aeroflow’s shop.
  • Spectra 9 Plus and Synergy Gold — named on Aeroflow’s Spectra category page as part of the Spectra family.
  • Lansinoh Thrive and Medela Pump In Style Pro+ (traditional and hands-free) — listed in Aeroflow’s shop.
  • Baby Buddha and Freemie — on Aeroflow’s hands-free comparison chart; OEM flange lists still to verify.
  • Momcozy, Pumpables, and other aftermarket or wearable makers — in scope; ship a page when that brand’s own size or compatibility list can be opened.
Aftermarket insert and flange makers (Maymom, Nenesupply, Pumpin’ Pal, LacTeck, BeauGen, and others) are part of the catalog too, as compatibility pages, not as “equivalent to OEM” claims. Seller-stated fit only, with milk-contact notes where the community raises them.
